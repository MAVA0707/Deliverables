# Client Briefs

## Case 1

A retail company wants to improve customer engagement inside large shopping malls. The company plans to install AI powered camera systems that analyze customer reactions in real time and adjust nearby digital advertising screens dynamically. The system would estimate emotional reactions such as stress, excitement, or frustration to maximize conversion rates and impulse purchases. Customers are not expected to actively interact with the system. The company believes this could significantly increase sales performance during peak shopping periods.

---

## Case 2

A financial institution wants to modernize its private loan approval process using AI assisted decision support. The proposed system evaluates customer financial data, spending patterns, debt exposure, and additional behavioral indicators to estimate repayment reliability. Most applications would be processed automatically, while human employees would mainly review unclear or borderline cases. The bank expects shorter processing times and more consistent lending decisions.

---

## Case 3

A customer support company plans to deploy an AI assistant on its website to handle incoming support requests. The chatbot should answer common questions, summarize technical problems, and guide users through troubleshooting steps before escalation to a human support employee. Users can request human assistance at any time if the AI cannot resolve the issue. The company mainly wants to reduce response times and improve service availability outside office hours.

---

## Case 4

A marketing agency wants to use generative AI tools internally to support creative campaign development. Employees would use the system to generate first draft social media captions, brainstorming ideas, and alternative wording suggestions for advertising campaigns. All final content would still be reviewed and approved by human team members before publication. The agency sees the system mainly as a productivity and inspiration tool.
