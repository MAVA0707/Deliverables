# Grind Guide

Choose grind size by brew method first, then adjust for taste.

## Grind Sizes

- `Extra fine`: Turkish coffee
- `Fine`: espresso, moka pot
- `Medium-fine`: AeroPress, some pour over
- `Medium`: pour over, drip
- `Medium-coarse`: immersion with shorter contact time
- `Coarse`: French press, cold brew

## Adjustment Heuristics

- If the coffee tastes sour or under-extracted, grind finer or extend brew time.
- If the coffee tastes bitter or over-extracted, grind coarser or shorten brew time.
- If the brew stalls or drips too slowly, grind coarser.
- If the brew tastes weak but the ratio is correct, check grind, temperature, and pour technique before adding more coffee.

## Safety Net

- When uncertain, choose one notch coarser for immersion methods.
- When uncertain for espresso, start slightly coarser than "powdery" fine and dial in from there.
