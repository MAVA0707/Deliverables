# Brew Ratios

Use these as starting points, then adjust to taste and equipment.

## Common Methods

| Method | Coffee : Water | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Espresso | 1:2 | Use fine grind, measured dose, and short extraction |
| AeroPress | 1:12 to 1:16 | Bloom optional; adjust steep time and dilution |
| Pour over | 1:15 to 1:17 | Good all-purpose range for clean cups |
| French press | 1:12 to 1:15 | Use coarse grind and full immersion |
| Americano | 1:2 espresso shot + hot water to taste | Pull espresso first, then dilute |

## Quick Conversions

- `15 g` coffee at `1:15` = `225 g` water
- `18 g` coffee at `1:16` = `288 g` water
- `20 g` coffee at `1:12` = `240 g` water

## Rule Of Thumb

- Start with the middle of the range.
- Strengthen by using slightly more coffee or less water.
- Lighten by using slightly less coffee or more water.
