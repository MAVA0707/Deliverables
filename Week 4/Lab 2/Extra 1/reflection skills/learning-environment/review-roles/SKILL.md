# Review Roles

Use this skill to pressure-test your own work with respectful, specific critique.

## Procedure

1. Run at least two passes with labeled headings.
2. Choose the passes that fit the workflow.
3. Cite lines, excerpts, or reasoning steps when text, code, or an argument is present.
4. End with questions back to the user, not only assertions.
5. Keep the review specific and respectful.

## Suggested Passes

Use what fits the task:

- `Misconception scan`: identify assumptions, blind spots, or places where the work could be wrong
- `Constructive challenge`: argue the strongest respectful counterpoint
- `Reviewer checklist`: short pass over clarity, correctness, completeness, and next steps

## Optional Checklist

Use this only when it helps:

- Does the work say what it means clearly?
- What could a smart reviewer challenge first?
- What is missing, fragile, or under-justified?
- What should be revised before shipping or asking for help?

## Output Shape

Prefer this format:

- `Pass`: findings and verdict
- `Pass`: findings and verdict
- `Questions back to you`: what the reviewer wants the user to clarify or reconsider
- `Overall`: merged assessment and next step

## Rules

- Do not overdo the number of passes; use two or three, not a pile of labels.
- Be concrete and specific instead of vaguely negative.
- If the work is code or text, cite the exact line, snippet, or reasoning step when possible.
- End with at least one question that invites reflection or revision.
